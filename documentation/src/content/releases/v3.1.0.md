# v3.1.0: New Jecs and Promise diagnostics

`v3.1.0` adds four diagnostics for Promise rejection handling, Jecs component operations and queries, and native Roblox collection copies. It also updates assertion-comment matching for `oxlint-tsgolint` 7.0.2002 and moves the existing Jecs query rule to its new public name.

## ⚠️ Breaking Changes

- **prefer-single-world-query-in-jecs**: Replaces the public rule key `prefer-single-world-query` with `prefer-single-world-query-in-jecs`, with no compatibility alias; change `small-rules/prefer-single-world-query` to `small-rules/prefer-single-world-query-in-jecs` in your Oxlint configuration - by @howmanysmall in [#66](https://github.com/howmanysmall/small-rules/pull/66)

## 🚀 Added

- **no-discarded-rejection**: Reports empty or `undefined`-returning `.catch()` handlers on provable native Promises, such as `Promise.resolve(value).catch(() => undefined)`, while leaving custom Promise-like values, handlers that record or rethrow failures, and shadowed `undefined` alone - by @howmanysmall in [#66](https://github.com/howmanysmall/small-rules/pull/66)
- **no-has-before-remove-in-jecs**: Reports a matching `World.has(entity, Component)` guard around `World.remove(entity, Component)` because Jecs handles absent components, and autofixes stable calls to the direct removal - by @howmanysmall in [#66](https://github.com/howmanysmall/small-rules/pull/66)
- **prefer-membership-filter-in-jecs**: Reports queried Jecs components whose values are not consumed and moves them into `.with(...)`, for example `query(Visual, Transform)` becomes `query(Visual).with(Transform)`; it leaves stored queries, rest bindings, and side-effect-sensitive expressions unchanged - by @howmanysmall in [#66](https://github.com/howmanysmall/small-rules/pull/66)
- **prefer-native-collection-copy**: Reports proven shallow-copy loops for `Map` and `Set` and autofixes them to `table.clone(source)`, while skipping weak collections, opaque iterables, filtered or transformed copies, and shadowed `table` - by @howmanysmall in [#66](https://github.com/howmanysmall/small-rules/pull/66)

## 🐞 Fixed

- **require-safety-comment-for-type-assertion**: Matches described `oxlint-disable`, `oxlint-disable-line`, and `oxlint-disable-next-line` directives against the type-assertion syntax when `oxlint-tsgolint` is `7.0.2002` or newer, while retaining enclosing-statement matching for older versions; the version can come from `settings["small-rules"].tsgolintVersion` or project metadata - by @howmanysmall in [#64](https://github.com/howmanysmall/small-rules/pull/64)
