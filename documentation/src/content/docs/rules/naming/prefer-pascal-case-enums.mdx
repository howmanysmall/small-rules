---
title: "Prefer Pascal Case Enums"
---

import RulePage from "$components/rule-page.astro";

<RulePage rule="prefer-pascal-case-enums">
	<Fragment slot="rationale">
		<p>
			Enums are types, and types are Pascal case. The members follow suit, so you write `Direction.Up` instead of
			`Direction.UP` or `direction.up`. That's also how Roblox's own enums look, like `Enum.KeyCode.LeftShift` and
			`Enum.Material.SmoothPlastic`, so your enums read like the ones you use next to them.
		</p>
		<p>
			The rule skips member names that start with a digit, or that use characters past A to Z, because there's
			no Pascal case spelling to suggest.
		</p>
	</Fragment>
</RulePage>
