---
title: "Require Async Suffix"
---

import RulePage from "$components/rule-page.astro";

<RulePage rule="require-async-suffix">
	<Fragment slot="rationale">
		<p>
			An async function returns a promise, and nothing at the call site shows that unless the name does. With the
			suffix, `loadProfileAsync()` without an `await` in front of it stands out on its own, and you don't have to
			hover the function to find out.
		</p>
		<p>
			It backs off where you don't get to pick the name: methods marked `override`, and methods on an object whose
			shape comes from elsewhere. That covers objects passed straight into a call or `new`, checked with
			`satisfies`, or annotated with an imported type. Anything else you need to keep, put in `except`.
		</p>
	</Fragment>
</RulePage>
