---
title: "Prevent Abbreviations"
---

import RulePage from "$components/rule-page.astro";

<RulePage rule="prevent-abbreviations">
	<Fragment slot="rationale">
		<p>
			`btn`, `cb`, `ctx`, `evt`: every abbreviation is one more thing
			a reader has to expand in their head, and not everyone expands them the same way. Spell the word out. When
			there's one obvious replacement, the rule can rename it for you everywhere it's used. When there's more than
			one, like `e`, which could be `error` or `event`, it lists them and lets
			you pick.
		</p>
		<p>
			Properties aren't checked by default, since a lot of them belong to Roblox or to a library and you can't
			rename them. Names imported from packages are left alone for the same reason. If your team really does treat
			a short name as a word, add it to `allowList`.
		</p>
	</Fragment>
</RulePage>
