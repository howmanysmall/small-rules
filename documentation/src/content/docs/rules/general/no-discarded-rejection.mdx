---
title: "No Discarded Rejection"
---

import RulePage from "$components/rule-page.astro";

<RulePage rule="no-discarded-rejection">
	<Fragment slot="rationale">
		<p>
			An empty catch turns a Promise that didn't work into a Promise whose value is `undefined`. The miss is
			hidden. You do not log it, and you do not give a fallback.
		</p>
		<p>
			The rule warns on the empty forms. It does not add a throw. It only follows a Promise it can see from the
			syntax and the scope. To hide one on purpose, use an `oxlint-disable` comment and say why.
		</p>
	</Fragment>
</RulePage>
