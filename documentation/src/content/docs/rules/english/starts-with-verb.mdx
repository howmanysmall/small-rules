title: "Starts With Verb"
---

import RulePage from "$components/rule-page.astro";

<RulePage rule="starts-with-verb">
	<Fragment slot="rationale">
		<p>
			A function does something, so its name should say what. `getUserProfile()` reads as an action at the call
			site; `userProfile()` reads like a value, and you have to check whether it is one.
		</p>
		<p>
			Names with no lowercase prefix, such as `UserProfile` components, are left alone. It also backs off where
			you don't get to pick the name: `override` members, getters and setters, constructors, computed keys, and
			methods on an object you pass straight into a call, a `new`, or a `satisfies`. Add your own words with
			`allowList`, and reject words with `denyList`.
		</p>
	</Fragment>
</RulePage>
