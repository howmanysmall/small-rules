title: "Starts With Verb"
---

import RulePage from "$components/rule-page.astro";

<RulePage rule="starts-with-verb">
	<Fragment slot="rationale">
		<p>
			A function does something, so its name should say what. `getUserProfile()` reads as an action at the call
			site; `userProfile()` reads like a value, and you have to check whether it's one.
		</p>
		<p>
			It leaves names with no lowercase prefix alone, such as `UserProfile` components. It also backs off where
			you don't get to pick the name: `override` members, getters and setters, constructors, computed keys, and
			keys of an object whose shape comes from elsewhere. That covers objects passed straight into a call or
			`new`, checked with `satisfies`, or annotated with an imported type. Add your own words with `allowList`,
			and exclude words with `denyList`.
		</p>
	</Fragment>
</RulePage>
