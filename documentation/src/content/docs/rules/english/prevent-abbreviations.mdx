---
title: "Prevent Abbreviations"
---

import RulePage from "$components/rule-page.astro";

<RulePage rule="prevent-abbreviations">
	<Fragment slot="rationale">
		<p>
			`btn`, `cb`, `ctx`, `evt`: every abbreviation is one more thing a reader has to expand in their head, and
			not everyone expands them the same way. Spell the word out. When there's one clear replacement, the rule can
			rename it for you everywhere it's used. When there's more than one, like `e`, which could be `error` or
			`event`, it lists them and lets you pick.
		</p>
		<p>
			Properties aren't checked by default, since a lot of them belong to Roblox or to a library and you can't
			rename them. The rule leaves names imported from packages alone for the same reason. If your team does treat
			a short name as a word, add it to `allowList`.
		</p>
	</Fragment>
</RulePage>
