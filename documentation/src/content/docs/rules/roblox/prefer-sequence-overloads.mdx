---
title: "Prefer Sequence Overloads"
---

import RulePage from "$components/rule-page.astro";

<RulePage rule="prefer-sequence-overloads">
	<Fragment slot="rationale">
		<p>
			`new ColorSequence(a, a)` is just `new ColorSequence(a)` with extra typing and overhead. Same for
			`NumberSequence`. And `new ColorSequence([a, b])` is the two-keypoint array form when `new ColorSequence(a,
			b)` exists and is FAR cheaper to run AND type.
		</p>
		<p>
			This collapses both cases into the shorter overload. Auto-fixes when the keypoint values match what the
			overload expects. Works for `ColorSequence` and `NumberSequence` -- anything with a `Keypoint` type.
		</p>
	</Fragment>
</RulePage>
