---
title: "No `print`"
---

import RulePage from "$components/rule-page.astro";

<RulePage rule="no-print">
	<Fragment slot="rationale">
		<p>
			Unnecessary output logging is a bad habit. `print()` is a raw Luau function with no log levels, no
			timestamps, and no filtering. Once it's in production you can't turn it off or route it anywhere useful.
		</p>
		<p>
			Use <a href="https://npmx.dev/package/@rbxts/rbxts-sleitnick-log">Log</a> instead. It gives you levels,
			among other things. Not recommended to suppress this one.
		</p>
	</Fragment>
</RulePage>
