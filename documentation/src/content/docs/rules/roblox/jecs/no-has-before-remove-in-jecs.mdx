---
title: "No Has Before Remove In Jecs"
---

import RulePage from "$components/rule-page.astro";

<RulePage rule="no-has-before-remove-in-jecs">
	<Fragment slot="rationale">
		<p>
			Jecs already allows `World.remove` when the entity does not have the component. A matching `World.has` guard
			adds branching without changing that result.
		</p>
		<p>
			The rule follows `world()` imports from `@rbxts/jecs`; it does not infer a World from a variable named
			`world`. It reports matching guards even when repeated argument evaluation prevents a safe fix.
		</p>
	</Fragment>
</RulePage>
