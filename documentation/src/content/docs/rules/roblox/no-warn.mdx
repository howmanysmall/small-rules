---
title: "No `warn`"
---

import RulePage from "$components/rule-page.astro";

<RulePage rule="no-warn">
	<Fragment slot="rationale">
		<p>
			Same reasoning as `print()`. `warn()` is just `print()` with a different output channel: no levels, no
			filtering, useless in production.
		</p>
		<p>
			Use [`Log`](https://npmx.dev/package/@rbxts/rbxts-sleitnick-log) instead. It handles severity through its
			API, not through which global you called. Not recommended to suppress this one either.
		</p>
	</Fragment>
</RulePage>
